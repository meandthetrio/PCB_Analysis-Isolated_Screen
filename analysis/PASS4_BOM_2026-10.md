## 3. Findings

### F-063 — VIOLATION, confirmed: C17 is on two BOM lines
Line 8: "100nF 25V X7R 0603" (C1590) lists `C1, C4, C5, C8, C11, C14, C16, C17`. Line 15: "100uF 35V 6.3×7.7 electrolytic" (C3339) lists `C6, C7, C15, C17, C18, …, C22`. The design's C17 is the **100 µF electrolytic** on the Daisy VIN RC ladder (Net-(C17-Pad1), footprint CP_Elec_6.3x7.7). Line 8's C17 is a stale entry — in Round 1 the 100 nF line read `…C11, C16, C17` and the electrolytic line did not include C17, so the designator was re-used when the power section was redrawn and the old line was never cleaned. Consequence at JLCPCB: a duplicate designator is either rejected at BOM upload or resolved to the first occurrence, which would try to place a 0603 chip on a 6.3 mm electrolytic footprint. **Fix:** delete C17 from line 8.

### F-064 — VIOLATION, confirmed (refines F-045 and F-003): three BOM designators have no position
- **U5 = L7805CDT-TR** (LCSC C310413), a DPAK 5 V linear regulator. Nothing in the schematic or board is called U5, and nothing needs a 5 V rail (the display runs on the TPS62172's 3.3 V). Most likely an earlier plan for the OLED supply that the buck replaced; the line survived.
- **S1, S2 = XUNPU TS-1002S 6 × 6 SMD tactile** (C2835239) — unchanged from Round 1: the board's switches are all through-hole. Stale.
Three placements with no CPL coordinates → JLCPCB flags them as "parts not found in placement file"; harmless if someone answers the DFM question, a stalled order if nobody does. **Fix:** delete lines 27 and 32.

### F-002 / F-034 — persist: LED1 and LED2 are still the only SMD parts missing from the BOM
CLS6B-FKW, top side, with F_Paste still carrying their 12 apertures. Together with F-052 (wired reverse-biased), the LED subsystem remains unplaced *and* non-functional. If LEDs are wanted this round (Q-R2-4), the BOM needs a line **and** the drive needs the F-052 fix first.

### F-065 — JUDGMENT, plausible: L1 land pattern is for the wrong body size
BOM and schematic both say DFE201610P-2R2M (Murata, 2.0 × 1.6 × 1.0 mm). The footprint is KiCad's generic `L_0805_2012Metric` (2.0 × 1.25 body; pads 0.88 × 1.20 mm at ±1.06). The part is 0.35 mm wider than the pad height, so its terminals overhang the pads on the long sides. It will usually solder, but with reduced fillet and a tombstone/skew risk on a part carrying the buck's switching current. Murata's recommended land for the DFE201610 could not be fetched (murata.com not on the allow list); the 0806/2016 land pattern (≈ 0.9 × 1.8 mm pads at ±1.0) is the standard fix.

### F-026 — persists (JUDGMENT): no hand-assembly list, now 18 parts
A1 Daisy Seed, ENCL1/ENCR1, Headphones1 pot, J3–J7 jacks, **J8 OLED**, MK1, SW1, TAC_×4, and two through-hole capacitors at the display connector — **C23 100 nF axial** and **C24 100 µF radial** — that are easy to forget precisely because they look like SMT parts on the schematic. Recommend a second BOM sheet.
