# Pass 4 (Daisy Pod Rev5 reference) — Three-Way BOM Cross-Check
**Date:** 2026-10-12 · **Truth checked:** Electrosmith's published Eagle source (`ES_Daisy_Pod_Rev5.sch` / `.brd`, Eagle 9.6.2) and the Pod BOM; the KiCad 9.0.9 conversion (`daisy_pod/kicad/ES_Daisy_Pod_Rev5.kicad_pcb`) is used only for pad/paste/SMD-vs-THT facts, and the F/B paste layers were plotted from that conversion (`kicad_out/pod/gerbers/`). Part data: JLCPCB parts-search API (the only vendor catalogue reachable — see "Reachability"), Bourns PEC11J datasheet, onsemi NSR1020MW2 datasheet, TI TPA6110A2 datasheet (DGN package drawing + stencil example), Electrosmith `Pod_databrief-3-13.pdf`.
**Scripts:** `analysis/daisy_pod/scripts/pass4/` — `pod_join.py` (BOM ↔ sch ↔ brd ↔ pcbnew ↔ pos join, value/package checks, qty check, DNI and library-attribute checks), `paste_gerber.py` (D03 flashes **and** G36 regions attributed to pcbnew paste pads), `jlc_lookup.py` (MPN → JLCPCB/LCSC catalogue record) plus raw outputs `join.csv`, `bom_lines.csv`, `summary.txt`, `join_table.md`, `jlc_lookup.txt`. Working copies and downloaded datasheets stayed in the session scratchpad; nothing under `reference/` was modified.

## Verdict summary
- **The join is clean and complete.** 47 BOM designators ↔ 47 schematic parts ↔ 47 board elements, all three-way. Zero phantom (BOM-only) designators, zero designators on two lines, zero duplicate refs on the board, zero placed parts missing from either schematic or board. Everything on the board that is *not* on the BOM is expected: P2 (12-pin header, value `M12_ALL_CIRCLE_DNI`), H1–H4 (3.2 mm mounting holes), FD1–FD6 (1 mm fiducials, no paste), U$2/U$3/U$4/U$7 (silkscreen logos, no pads). The 27 schematic-only parts are power/frame symbols (23 GND, 2 +3V3_A, 2 A3L-LOC frames).
- **Every paste opening maps to a BOM part.** 118 top + 90 bottom openings; 104 + 89 are pad flashes matched 1:1 to pcbnew paste pads, the remaining 14 + 1 are G36 regions that are custom Eagle tCream graphics of BOM parts (VR_1/VR_2 pin-in-paste windows, P5 shell-tab windows, U4 PowerPAD window). The Pod has **no SMD part off the BOM** — the exact opposite of Trey's rev 3 (LED1/LED2).
- **Two BOM errors that matter for a build:** (1) **R78 and R84 are `DNI 0R` in schematic and board but are ordered and placed by BOM row 13** (0R ×4: R79 R83 R78 R84). They are the TRS-MIDI Type-A/Type-B polarity jumpers; populating all four shorts the MIDI jack's tip to its ring (DP-P4-01). (2) **U1 Samtec SSM-120-L-SV Qty 1 for a footprint that needs two** 1×20 sockets (40 pads in two rows) (DP-P4-02).
- **MPN sanity: 23/23 MPNs resolve** to the stated parts in the JLCPCB/LCSC catalogue or the manufacturer datasheet; the Cree LED's lowercase `a` is a case typo (real P/N `CLVBA-FKA-CAEDH8BBB7A363`, and a lowercase-`a` *customer placeholder* already exists in JLCPCB's database, so someone has tried to order with it); Bourns `PEC11J-9220F-H0015` decodes per the datasheet as SMD gull-wing / 30 detents / 20 mm flatted shaft / 400 gf switch / 15 PPR; the Song Huei pot exists only as customer-created placeholders (Thonk is blocked). Library attributes drift from the BOM on LED1/LED2 (a different Cree bin) and U7 (reel suffix) (DP-P4-03).
- **Values and packages: 47/47 consistent** across BOM IPN/MPN, schematic value, Eagle package and the pad geometry of the KiCad conversion — including the deliberate mixed sizes (six 1 kΩ LED resistors are 0402 and the three 100 nF are 0402 while every other passive is 0603), the TPA6110 MSOP-PowerPAD (100 % paste on the EP via a tCream rectangle equal to TI's stencil example), PLCC4 LEDs, the micro-USB `_CUT2` package (same Molex 47589-0001, different outline treatment), the Hirose SD socket and the 6.3×7.7 mm electrolytics (DP-P4-07).

## Method notes
- **BOM parser**: `openpyxl`, header asserted; designators split on whitespace/commas; Qty compared with designator count per line; duplicate-designator and whitespace checks on every cell.
- **Schematic parser**: `<parts>/<part>` joined to `<libraries>/<library>/<devicesets>/<deviceset>/<devices>/<device>` for the package and to `<technologies>/<technology>/<attribute>` for IPN/MPN/PACKAGE attributes; parts whose device has no package are classed schematic-only. Cross-checked against the existing `kicad_out/pod/sch_netlist.json` (same 85 parts, same packages).
- **Board parser**: `<elements>/<element>` (name, library, package, value, position, rotation, `<attribute>` overrides); packages inspected for `<smd cream="no">`, `<pad>`, `<hole>`, Dimension/Milling wires and tCream/bCream (layers 31/32) graphics.
- **KiCad side**: `pcbnew` iteration of footprints and pads (attribute SMD/PTH/NPTH, layer set incl. F/B.Paste, sizes, nets). Side/tech and paste counts in the join come from here; the paste Gerbers were parsed independently (`paste_gerber.py`, flashes + region centroids) and matched to pcbnew pad centres within 0.25 mm: F 104/104 flashes + 14 regions, B 89/89 + 1 region, every region explained by an Eagle cream polygon/rectangle of a BOM part.
- **Value checker**: for R/C refs the value token (`47K`, `100NF`…) must appear in the IPN and the IPN/MPN/package size codes must agree with the measured pad class (0402 vs 0603); for all other refs the IPN must equal the Eagle value (Electrosmith uses the IPN as the value) or the pair is listed as `CHECK` for hand review.
- **Reference land patterns**: Bourns PEC11J rev 06/26 datasheet pages 2–3 (bottom view, recommended PCB layout, rendered at 300 dpi and read off the drawing), TI `tpa6110a2.pdf` DGN0008D land-pattern and stencil pages, onsemi NSR1020MW2 ordering table. Cree, Alps, Molex, Hirose, Samtec and Chemi-Con drawings were not reachable; the JLCPCB catalogue record (package/size strings) stands in for them and is cited as such.
