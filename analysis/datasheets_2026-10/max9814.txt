General Description                                                 Features
The MAX9814 is a low-cost, high-quality microphone                  ● Automatic Gain Control (AGC)
amplifier with automatic gain control (AGC) and                     ● Three Gain Settings (40dB, 50dB, 60dB)
low-noise microphone bias. The device features a
low-noise preamplifier, variable gain amplifier (VGA), output       ● Programmable Attack Time
amplifier, microphone-bias-voltage generator, and AGC               ● Programmable Attack and Release Ratio
control circuitry.                                                  ● 2.7V to 5.5V Supply Voltage Range
The low-noise preamplifier has a fixed 12dB gain, while             ● Low Input-Referred Noise Density of 30nV/√Hz
the VGA gain automatically adjusts from 20dB to 0dB,
                                                                    ● Low THD: 0.04% (typ)
depending on the output voltage and the AGC threshold.
The output amplifier offers selectable gains of 8dB, 18dB,          ● Low-Power Shutdown Mode
and 28dB. With no compression, the cascade of the                   ● Internal Low-Noise Microphone Bias, 2V
amplifiers results in an overall gain of 40dB, 50dB, or 60dB.
                                                                    ● Available in the Space-Saving, 14-Pin TDFN
A trilevel digital input programs the output amplifier gain. An
                                                                      (3mm x 3mm) Package
external resistive divider controls the AGC threshold and
a single capacitor programs the attack/release times. A             ● -40°C to +85°C Extended Temperature Range
trilevel digital input programs the ratio of attack-to-release
time. The hold time of the AGC is fixed at 30ms. The low-
noise microphone-bias-voltage generator can bias most
electret microphones.                                               Ordering Information appears at end of data sheet.
The MAX9814 is available in the space-saving, 14-pin
TDFN package. This device is specified over the -40°C to
+85°C extended temperature range.
