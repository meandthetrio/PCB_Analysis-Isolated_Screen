                                           Audio Codec

Internal Codec Details

Codec                                                  PCM3060
Mode Control                                           H/W Mode, Single-ended VoutX
FMT (Interface Format)                                 24-bit left-justified for ADC and DAC
DEMP(De-emphasis)                                      De-emphasis off
IFMD (Interface Mode)                                  Slave mode for ADC, slave mode for DAC
Digital VDD                                            3.3V (Filtered from Daisy +3v3_D)
Analog VCC                                             4.5V provided by on board LDO


The STM32 SAI1 peripheral is used to communicate digital audio to the codec. The following pins are connect-
ed:

STM32 Pin Name                       STM32 Function                      PCM3060 Pin(s)

PE2                                  SAI1 MCLK A                         SCKI1 SCKI2

PE3                                  SAI1 SD B                           DOUT

PE4                                  SAI1 FS A                           LRCK1 LRCK2

PE5                                  SAI1 SCK A                          BCK1 BCK2

PE6                                  SAI1 SD A                           DIN


All three clocks coming from the STM32 SAI1 peripheral (FS, SCK, and MCLK) are shared between the ADC
and DAC on the PCM3060.

The Codec VCOM is filtered per datasheet recommended values, and is not exposed for use.
The PCM3060 is configured by the hardware, and is not connected via I2C for serial control.

                                          Hardware Revisions
The Daisy Seed has received a handful of hardware revisions over its lifetime. These revisions are a result of
deprecated parts, and massive chip shortages that have affected the global electronics supply chain. Regardless of
your revision, each Daisy Seed is pin to pin compatible with one another. See a full list of changes below for each
revision.

 Revision Active Years Changes                                   Reason For Revision

 Rev 4       2020-2021         None                              Original Kickstarter Seed

 Rev 5       2021-2023         Audio Codec - WM8731              AKM factory fire, AK4556 discontinued

 Rev 7       2023 -            Audio Codec - PCM3060             WM8731 discontinued


Notes:
- Rev4/Rev7 do not use I2C pins. Auto-software detects hardware as Rev 4 Daisy in libDaisy, so it can remain
backwards compatible with existing firmware. See table below for which pin each rev uses for its version check.
- For each STM32 Pin, the board’s “version pin” will be shorted to GND, and therefore unable to be pulled up.


 Revision STM32 Pin

 Rev 5       PD3

 Rev 7       PD5
