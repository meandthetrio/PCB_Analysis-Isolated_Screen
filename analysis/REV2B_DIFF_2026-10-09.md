## 3. New findings
- **F-076 (VIOLATION, confirmed):** the LED named in the schematic (Cree CLS6B-FKW) cannot be mounted on the board's footprint. Cree datasheet p. 1/9: 4.7 × 1.5 × 1.3 mm side-view package, six pins in one row across 2.82 mm. Board footprint (Round 1, 2 and 2b): KiCad `LED_RGB_5050-6` geometry, 5 × 5 mm PLCC6, two columns of three pads at x = ±2.4 mm, 1.7 mm pitch. Missed in Rounds 1 and 2 (pinout was checked, land pattern was not). Actual LED part in Trey's hands is unknown → Q-R2-9.
- **F-077 (VIOLATION, confirmed):** the rev2b low-side switches sink the LED **anodes**. Q collectors go to pins 1/3/5 (symbol pin functions AR/AG/AB; Cree pins 1/3/5 = anodes), the 300 Ω resistors to +9V_FLAG stay on pins 2/4/6 (cathodes). Same reverse bias as F-052, now through a transistor. Fix: swap the two groups — collectors to pins 2/4/6, 300 Ω from +9V_FLAG to pins 1/3/5 (six nets, no new parts). If the physical LED turns out to have the opposite pinout (Q-R2-9), the symbol is wrong instead and the copper is right; either way the symbol and the part must be reconciled before this is built.
