## Verdict summary
- **The join is clean and complete.** 130 production-BOM designators ↔ 130 CDN-BOM designators (identical set) ↔ 130 schematic symbols ↔ 130 board footprints, all three-way. The 9 schematic+board parts off the BOM (FD1–FD3, H1–H4, LOGO1, QR1) all carry the `exclude_from_bom` flag in **both** schematic and board. Zero phantom, PCB-only or schematic-only designators, zero duplicates, zero DNP anywhere (and the CDN `DNP` column is `FALSE` on all 42 lines, consistent). The netlist flags were validated against the raw `.kicad_sch` files: 139/139 refs, flag sets identical (EU-P4-06).
- **Every SMD part is on the BOM and every paste opening maps to it.** 272 F.Paste flashes in both the session plot and the 2026-06-18 production Gerber, 272/272 matched to the 98 SMD BOM parts' pads, 0 unmatched, no B.Paste (single-sided assembly). **No SMD part is off the BOM** — the opposite of Trey's rev 3 (LED1/LED2) and the same as the Pod (EU-P4-09).
- **The 32 through-hole parts are on the BOM too, marked `Assembly = PTH`** (A1 Seed 3, 15 jacks, P1, LED1, LED2, SW1–SW4, U11 DIP-6 optocoupler, VR1–VR8) — the kit BOM is a complete assembly BOM, not just an SMT sheet (EU-P4-10).
- **Values and packages: 130/130 consistent** (BOM footprint = schematic footprint = board footprint on every line; CDN Package/Assembly consistent with pad type on all 42 lines; pad classes match the stated sizes) (EU-P4-07).
- **What is wrong is documentation-grade, not build-grade:** (1) the production BOM splits R78 onto its own line because its value is `100k` (lowercase) while the other nine 100 kΩ are `100K` (EU-P4-01); (2) the CDN BOM's MPN column quietly substitutes generic parts for the parts its CPN column names — Shikues `B5817WS` for `NSR1020MW2T3G` (D1–D3), an "HXY MOSFET" `USBLC6-2SC6` for ST's, a HOAUC `HYCW445-USBC16-730B` for GCT's `USB4110-GF-A` — on lines whose `Sub OK` column says `NO` (EU-P4-02); (3) the usual hand-maintained-sheet hygiene (EU-P4-03/04); (4) the project folder carries leftovers of the Pedal kit it was cloned from — `Seed3-Pedal-DevKit-Rev1.csv`, a 6.5 MB `Seed3-Pedal-DevKit-Converted-eagle-import.kicad_sym`, lib-table entries for Pedal-only relay/USB libraries, and absolute `/Users/stephenhensley/…` Eagle library paths (EU-P4-05).
- **Seed 3 is a direct-solder, through-hole line item** (`A1`, CDN package `SOM`, `Assembly = PTH`, no vendor): stock KiCad `MCU_Module:Electrosmith_Daisy_Seed_Rev4` symbol and `Module:Electrosmith_Daisy_Seed` footprint, 2×20 PTH 1.7 mm pads / 1.1 mm drills on 2.54 mm pitch, rows 15.24 mm apart, 48.26 mm span — exactly the Seed 3 datasheet landing pattern (p.9, which itself links to the KiCad part). No socket or header appears on the BOM or the board (EU-P4-11).
