## 1. Board
- **Outline:** 8 lines + 4 arcs (r = 2.5) on Edge.Cuts, 0.05 mm stroke → **141.95 × 128.55 mm rounded rectangle**, one outline, **no cutouts**, 1.6 mm, area 18,111.2 mm². No footprint-level Edge.Cuts. The production `Edge_Cuts.gm1` (12 draws) is identical. The gbrjob says 141.95 × 128.55; the README gives no board dimensions, only "28HP / 3U".
- **28HP / 3U check** (Doepfer figures from memory, see header): 28 × 5.08 − 0.3 = **141.94** ≈ 141.95 ✓; 3U panel height 128.5 ≈ 128.55 (+0.05) ✓. The PCB *is* the panel: every one of the 139 footprints is on **F** (B side carries only "(C) Daisy 2026" and "Rev3" silk and the GND pour), so the component face is the user face and the B side faces the case ("2 mm behind rails").
- Front-face layout (F): power header P1 and the +12/−12 V entry (D1/D2, FB2/FB3, C39/C44, C50/C51) top-left under the OSHW logo / QR code; U11 opto + MIDI column at x = 110.49 (MIDI IN 41.4, THRU 62.5, OUT 83.8); 8 pots in two rows (y 40.5 / 61.6) × 4 columns (x 127.95 / 145.42 / 162.88 / 180.34, **17.46 mm pitch** = 11/16"); 12 jacks in three rows (y 83.78 / 104.89 / 126.0, **21.11 pitch**) in the same 4 columns; CV-in signal conditioning (U2, U12 mux, U3 ref) between pots and jacks; the Seed A1 across the bottom-left (49.77–102.83 × 99.5–118.3); micro-SD U7 and USB-C J7 on the left edge; LED1 (RGB) / LED2 and SW1–SW4 along the bottom (y 123–144, 17.46 pitch).

## 3. Courtyards (EU-P6-04)

Not a panel-mounted overlap: A1 is the `Module:Electrosmith_Daisy_Seed` THT footprint (40 × 1.1 mm holes), the BOM lists "A1 — Seed3, SOM, PTH" and no socket, so the Seed is soldered in on its own male headers; the header body holds the module ≥ 2.5 mm above the board and a 0603 is 0.45 mm tall. Physically fine and clearly deliberate (the 47 k pull-downs for the Seed's D-pins); it is a courtyard-convention conflict only. JUDGMENT. The same five parts overlap A1 on the Pedal kit. **vs rev 3:** 0 missing, 0 overlaps (R3-P6-03). **vs Pod:** 21 missing (Eagle conversion), 0 overlaps.

## 5. Mounting — the Eurorack panel pattern (EU-P6-07)
H1–H4 `shnsly:MNT_SLOT_M3_PLATED`: **plated slots 6.4 × 3.2 mm with a 7.0 × 3.8 copper pad (ring 0.30) and a 1:1 mask window, no net** (pads report "unconnected-(Hx-Pad1)" = a no-connect pin on the schematic), at **(57.5, 29.5), (57.5, 152.0), (184.4, 29.5), (184.4, 152.0)** → **3.025 mm from the top/bottom edge, 7.525 mm from the left/right edge, 122.5 mm apart vertically, 126.9 mm apart horizontally.** Against the Doepfer A-100 panel rule (from memory — see header): holes 3.0 mm from the top and bottom edges ✓ (3.025), 7.5 mm in from the side ✓ (7.525), vertical pitch 122.5 ✓, horizontal pitch (HP − 3) × 5.08 = 127.0 (measured 126.9, i.e. the right pair is 0.1 inboard — inside the slot's 1.6 mm of play); M3 rail screws through 3.2 mm-wide slots ✓. The slots' long axis is horizontal (6.4 along x), which is what rail-spacing tolerance needs. Copper pad + plating around a panel screw on the GND-pour side: the pad has no net and the pour keeps the netclass clearance from it, so the rails are not tied to GND through the screws (fine for a panel; the jacks' sleeves do that). Nearest via 10.2–56.6 mm, nearest footprint ≥ 6.9 mm (fiducials). PASS on the measurements; the ruling against the Doepfer figures stays *plausible* until that page can be fetched. **vs rev 3:** no mounting holes at all (F-032 / R3-P6-05). **vs Pod:** 4 × M3 3.2 PTH with 5.84 pads.
