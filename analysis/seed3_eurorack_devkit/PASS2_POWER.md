Answers to the scoping questions:
- **Power entry / reverse polarity.** Each rail has its own series Schottky (D1 on +12 V, D2 on −12 V, NSR1020 VR 20 V) behind a ferrite. A reversed ribbon swaps +12 and −12 on the header while GND stays on GND; both diodes then see ≤ 12 V reverse against VR = 20 V and nothing conducts. "Reverse power protection circuit is installed" (README) is true by construction. There is no bridge and no shunt crowbar. The bus +5 V is not used (the header pattern is 2 + 6 + 2, no +5 V pins).
- **What makes +3V3 / +5V.** Nothing on the kit: there is **no regulator on the board**. +3V3 and +3V3_A are the Seed3's own outputs; every op-amp runs on the raw ±12 V; the only reference is the LM4040 −10 V shunt. DSY_VIN is +12 V minus FB2, D1 and the two 3.3 Ω.
- **Series elements from the bus to the Seed:** FB2 (0.2 Ω) → D1 → R5 3.3 Ω → R9 3.3 Ω = the datasheet's Fig 1.2 circuit, component for component (two 100 µF, two 3R3).
- **Codec rails:** TAC5242 lives on the Seed3 (AVDD/IOVDD 3.0–3.6 V from the module regulators). The kit only drives the module's AUDIO IN/OUT pins 16–19.
- **± rails for the CV op-amps:** U13 (CV out) and U1/U15 (audio) on ±12 V; U2 (CV in) on **+3V3_A only**, which is what bounds the ADC pins (§6).

The schematic draws the power block on the "Power" sheet exactly as datasheet Fig 1.2; the only sheet notes in the whole project are on the CV-input sheet: "Cutoff = 1 / (2piRC) = 4.822kHz" and "Range = -5V to 5V => 3V3 to 0V".

## 2. Current budget, series-element loading, inrush (`kit_power_budget.py euro`)

Loads: TL072 ×3 = 6 amplifiers → 8.4 mA typ / **15 mA max** from each of +12 V and −12 V (no output load; CV-out and audio-out load currents are ≤ 5 V / 100 Ω into a short = 50 mA worst case per output, normally µA into 100 kΩ Eurorack inputs); LM4040 string (12 − 10) V / 2 kΩ = 1.0 mA from −12 V; Seed3 VIN current **not published** → 0.10 / 0.20 / 0.30 A assumed (rev-3 PASS2 used 0.10–0.20 A; 0.30 A covers SD + USB device). +3V3 loads (Seed-sourced, not on the ±12 V budget): SD card bursts, H11L1 + 270 Ω (12 mA when the opto is low), two 10 Ω MIDI sources (≈ 9 mA each only while a loop is closed), 5 × 47 k, 2 × 10 k → < 0.25 A. +3V3_A: 8 × 10 k pots = 2.6 mA + CD4051 ≈ 10 µA + MCP6004 ≈ 0.4 mA.

- DSY_VIN 9.5–11.0 V is inside 4–17 V with ≥ 2.4 V of margin to the ceiling and ≥ 5.5 V to the floor. Note the flip side the datasheet itself raises (l.356–358): at ~10–11 V in, the Seed's regulators dissipate roughly 1.6× what they would from a 9 V pedal supply.
- LM4040-10: IR = 1.0 mA minus the four 200 k offset loads (233 µA) = **767 µA vs IRMIN 100 µA → 7.7×**; at −12 V −10 % (10.8 V) still 167 µA. PASS.
- **Inrush.** +12 V side 310 µF total, of which **110 µF (C39 + C50 + C2) sit behind only FB2 (0.2 Ω) + D1**; C46 is behind 3.3 Ω (3.5 A peak into R5, a single pulse into a 0.75 W 1206), C49 behind 6.6 Ω (1.7 A). −12 V side 110 µF behind FB3 + D2. Stored energy 30 mJ at ±12 V. Peak from a stiff bus ≈ (12 − 0.5) / (0.2 Ω + ESR) ≈ 30–40 A — above D1's 5 A (8.3 ms) surge rating on paper — but a Eurorack bus is not stiff: the ribbon, the bus-board and the supply's current limit set the real pulse, and this is the stock Electrosmith circuit. **JUDGMENT (EU-P2-03)**, same class as rev-3 R3-P2-05; the kit has about half of Trey's unlimited capacitance (110 vs 200 µF) and 4× the bead DCR, and no NTC either.
- Series-element placement (`§E`): FB2 (67.9, 56.3) → D1 (69.9, 56.4) → C50 (73.7) → C39 (79.1): the whole entry block is within 11 mm of the header pads; R5/R9 and C46/C49 sit 30 mm further down the left edge at (63.6, 85.9–93.5) / (57.0, 87.3–94.8), 7.9 mm from A1.39. I.e. the datasheet's Fig 1.2 is laid out as two blocks: filter at the header, π-section at the Seed.

## 5. LED drive (`kit_power_budget.py` LED section)

**Topology.** LED1 = Kingbright WP154A4SUREQBFZGW 5 mm RGB on `LED_D5.0mm-4_RGB_Wide_Pins`, symbol `LED_RKBG`: pad 2 (common cathode) → GND; pads 1/4/3 (R/G/B anodes) ← R40 / R25 / R44 **2 kΩ 0603** ← Seed A1.10 (D9) / A1.35 (D28) / A1.9 (D8). LED2 = WP7113ID red 5 mm: pad 1 → GND, pad 2 ← R43 2 kΩ ← A1.12 (D11). **GPIO sources** (push-pull high) — the opposite polarity to the Pod (common anode on 3V3_D, GPIO sinks) and to Trey's rev 3 (NPN low-side switches), with no transistor and no separate rail. None of pins 9/10/12/35 is on the 3.3 V-only list. Runs: 14.7–33.4 mm, 1–2 vias.

## 6. Buttons, toggles, pots + mux, CV in, gate in, CV out

**Buttons / toggles** (`§D`): TAC_SW1 (SW1 pads 1+2 → A1.1 = D0, 59.8–66.3 mm, 1 via), TAC_SW2 (SW2 → A1.27 = D20, 61.4–67.9 mm, 2 vias), TOG_SW3_A/B (SW3 ON-OFF-ON pins O/S → A1.34 = D27 / A1.8 = D7, 93.4 / 86.9 mm, 2 vias, 0.2 mm min width on _A), TOG_SW4 (SW4 ON-ON pin O → A1.13 = D12, 95.0 mm, 0 vias; pin S unconnected). Every one is a bare 2-/3-node net: **no external pull-up, no RC, no series resistor**, switch commons to GND. The datasheet's own text (l.559–567) says the pin is "configured … with a pull up resistor" in software and that an external 10 k is needed only "if we didn't have the pull up resistors"; Fig 2.3 draws the 10 k variant. The kit uses the internal pull-up — same as the Pod (DP-P2-08) and Trey (R3-P3-03), with runs 1.5× the Pod's. JUDGMENT (EU-P2-10). README pin map verified for all four (D0, D20, D27/D7, D12).

**Pots.** VR1–VR8 Alpha RV09 10 k B: pin 3 → +3V3_A, pin 1 → GND, wiper → U12 X0–X7 (POT_1 63.2 mm … POT_8 99.9 mm, 1–2 vias). U12 CD4051BM96: X → A1.22 (ADC0 = D15, `POT_MUX`, 15.3 mm, 0 vias); A/B/C ← A1.31/32/33 (D24/25/26, 37.9–45.3 mm); INH, VEE, VSS → GND; VDD → +3V3_A with C6 at 1.4 mm. 3.3 V is inside the CD4051B's 3–18 V recommended range, with the datasheet's caveat that performance "degrades for VDD < 3V" and no published rON at 3.3 V (the 125 Ω figure is at 15 V): the ADC sees 10 k pot ∥ + several hundred ohms of switch — a sampling-time matter for firmware, not a hardware finding. No RC anywhere on the pot chain; 2.6 mA total. Matches datasheet Fig 4.2. PASS (EU-P2-11). README pin map verified (D15, D24–D26).

**CV inputs** (4 identical channels; J_CV_IN_n tip → R33/R37/R41/R46 100 k → U2 inverting node; feedback R34/R38/R42/R47 33 k ∥ C14/C15/C16/C18 1 nF; R35/R36/R39/R45 200 k from −10V_REF; U2 MCP6004 on +3V3_A; non-inverting inputs → GND; outputs → A1.25/24/23/26 = D18/D17/D16/D19, 15.7–26.3 mm, 0 vias; jack sleeves → GND). This is datasheet Fig 4.3 verbatim (100K / 33K / 200K / 1NF / LM4040-10 / MCP6004 at 3.3 V):

| V_in | ideal V_out = −0.33·V_in + 1.65 V | op-amp output (rails 0 / 3.3 V) | summing node | current through the 100 k |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −5 V | +3.30 | 3.30 V | 0.00 V | 50 µA |
| 0 V | +1.65 | 1.65 V | 0.00 V | 0 |
| +5 V | 0.00 | 0.00 V | 0.00 V | 50 µA |

- ±5 V maps to 3.3 → 0 V (inverted, 0 V in = 1.65 V), exactly the README's "CV input range −5 V to +5 V" and the sheet note. The ADC pins never see more than the op-amp's own supply, **+3V3_A** — so pins 24 and 25 (3.3 V-only, carrying CV_2 and CV_1) are bounded by construction. PASS (EU-P2-12).
- Beyond ±5 V the output clamps at a rail and the inverting input leaves virtual ground: −1.1 V at −10 V in, −1.55 V at −12 V in. That is below the MCP6004's VSS by more than its input diode drop, so the input clamps and the 100 k limits the current to ≈ 90–105 µA — far under the ±2 mA abs-max input current (model memory, flagged). This is the "act as a clamp" behaviour the datasheet describes (l.1679–1683). Clamp margin: JUDGMENT-free PASS, conditional on the unfetched MCP6004 sheet.
- 33 k ∥ 1 nF → fc 4.82 kHz (sheet note "4.822kHz" ✓).

**Gate inputs** (2 identical: J_GATE_IN_n tip → R17/R24 100 k → Q1/Q2 MMBT3904 base; R18/R28 1 M base→GND; collector → R19/R30 10 k → +3V3 and → R23/R31 100 Ω → A1.11 (D10) / A1.28 (D21); emitters → GND). Datasheet Fig 2.4 verbatim (10K / 100R / 100K / 1M). Inverting: a gate above ≈ Vbe × (1 + 100 k / 1 M) + Ib·100 k ≈ **0.7–0.8 V** pulls the GPIO low (the README's "Gate input threshold +0.4 V" is lower than the arithmetic gives — documentation discrepancy, not a hardware issue). The GPIO never sees more than the 10 k pull-up's 3.3 V, so **pin 28 (3.3 V-only, GATE_2) is bounded**. A −12 V input reverse-biases the base-emitter junction: with the 1 M to ground the base would sit at −10.9 V, which exceeds VEBO 6 V, but the 100 k limits the current to (12 − 6) / 100 k ≈ 60 µA — the junction avalanches harmlessly at that current. PASS (EU-P2-13); runs 47.9 / 69.7 mm, 2 vias.

**CV outputs** (A1.30 = D23 DAC1_OUT1 → U13.3; A1.29 = D22 DAC1_OUT2 → U13.5; U13 TL072 on ±12 V non-inverting, feedback R49/R57 62 k, R48/R56 120 k to GND; → R55/R60 100 Ω → jack tip): gain 1 + 62/120 = **1.517 → 0…5.00 V** from the 0…3.3 V DAC — datasheet Fig 6.1 verbatim (120K / 62K / 100R; the datasheet draws an MCP6002 on +5 V, the kit uses a TL072 on ±12 V). TL072 input range (VCC−)+4 = −8 V … fine at 0–3.3 V; output 0–5 V is well inside VOM; outputs short-circuit protected; 100 Ω → 50 mA into a dead short. **Pins 29/30 (3.3 V-only)** drive JFET inputs with pA bias current and no path to ±12 V other than through the op-amp's own input stage. PASS (EU-P2-13). DAC_CV runs 38.5 / 40.0 mm, 1 via, 0.2 mm.

## 7. Audio (the codec is on the Seed3; the kit is the ±12 V line interface)

```
IN:  J_AUDIO_IN_L1 tip ──► R61 100k ──► U1B (−); R62 10k ∥ C4 330pF; U1.5 (+) → GND ──► R63 100R ──► /Daisy/CODEC_IN_L = A1.16
     J_AUDIO_IN_R1 tip ──► R64 100k ──► U1A … R65 / C17 … R66 100R ──► A1.17        (J_AUDIO_IN_R1.NORM ← L tip: "Input Right is normalled to Input Left" ✓)
OUT: A1.18 CODEC_OUT_L ──► R67 10k ──► U15A (−); R73 100k ∥ C21 33pF ──► R79 100R ──► J_AUDIO_OUT_L1 tip   (R68/R78/C22/R80 for R)
```
- Gain **−0.1** in, **−10** out: 10 Vpp (Eurorack nominal) → 1.0 Vpp at the codec = 0.35 Vrms = **−9 dBFS** against the datasheet's 0 dBFS = 1 Vrms; "headroom up to about 20 Vpp" → 2 Vpp, under the 3.6 Vpp abs max; the abs max is only reached at 36 Vpp in. Output: 0 dBFS → 28 Vpp demanded, clipped by the TL072 at ≈ ±10.5 V on ±12 V (≈ 21 Vpp) — the symmetric −9 dBFS point gives the 10 Vpp nominal. Input impedance 100 kΩ and output impedance 100 Ω as the README states; both poles 48 kHz.
- Decoupling: each TL072 has one 100 nF **across V+/V−** (C5, C3) at 6.5–8.3 mm and nothing to ground closer than the entry bank (§3, EU-P2-06). Every stage is DC-coupled; the Seed3's own input/output coupling (Table 1 footnote "AC coupled") does the blocking, and a DC voltage patched into an input simply offsets the inverting stage by −0.1× (12 V DC → −1.2 V, inside ±1.8 V).
- Codec-net runs: CODEC_IN_L/R 82.7 / 90.1 mm, CODEC_OUT_L/R 106.1 / 90.0 mm, 2 vias each, 10 mil — long but the sources are 100 Ω op-amp outputs (Pass 5 territory). PASS (EU-P2-15).

## 10. ERC / schematic hygiene

5 × `power_pin_not_driven` (error): A1.39 VIN, `#GND01`, J7 VBUS, `#PWR01`, `#PWR02` — DSY_VIN, GND, +5V_USB and the ±12 V rails are fed through diodes/beads/resistors with no PWR_FLAG. Same class as Trey's R3-P2-14 / rev-2 F-024 (and the Pedal kit *does* carry PWR_FLAGs). The remaining ~90 ERC items are `lib_symbol_*` / `footprint_link_*` noise from Electrosmith's private libraries. JUDGMENT (EU-P2-17).

## Summary (one line per finding)

- EU-P2-01 PASS — power entry is datasheet Fig 1.2 verbatim: per-rail 1 A bead + series NSR1020, 100 µF/10 µF/100 nF bank, 3R3-100 µF-3R3-100 µF into Seed VIN; reverse ribbon blocked by the two diodes; no regulator on the kit.
- EU-P2-02 PASS — DSY_VIN 9.5–11.0 V for 0.1–0.3 A (Seed current unpublished, assumed); beads/diodes ≤ 32 % of 1 A; 3R3 ≤ 40 % of 0.75 W; D1 ≤ 67 % of PF; LM4040 7.7× IRMIN.
- EU-P2-03 JUDGMENT — 110 µF per rail behind only bead + diode, 30 mJ, no inrush limiter (same class as R3-P2-05; half of Trey's unlimited capacitance, 4× his bead DCR).
- EU-P2-04 JUDGMENT — one 100 nF on +3V3 at the opto (97.6 mm routed from the Seed), none at the SD socket (124.7 mm): the Pod/Trey pattern.
- EU-P2-05 PASS — +3V3_A: two 100 nF at the mux and CV op-amp, no bulk, 3 mA load.
- EU-P2-06 JUDGMENT — TL072s bypassed with one 100 nF across V+/V− each; no cap to ground within 54–130 mm; TI asks for 0.1 µF from each supply pin to ground.
- EU-P2-07 PASS — 16/32 mil power copper, ≤ 32 % IPC utilisation, ≤ 0.25 Ω summed on any rail.
- EU-P2-08 JUDGMENT — LEDs sourced from GPIO through 2 k: red 0.7 mA, green/blue 0.17 mA; dimmer than the Pod, opposite polarity to Pod and Trey.
- EU-P2-09 ZERO-MARGIN — green/blue Vf ≈ 3.3 V vs a 3.3 V GPIO: lit only by the low-current Vf roll-off; Kingbright sheet unreachable.
- EU-P2-10 JUDGMENT — buttons/toggles unfiltered, internal pull-ups, 60–95 mm runs (Pod/Trey match).
- EU-P2-11 PASS — 8 pots via CD4051 at 3.3 V to ADC0, datasheet Fig 4.2; no RC.
- EU-P2-12 PASS — CV in = Fig 4.3: ±5 V → 3.3–0 V, ADC pins bounded by +3V3_A, ≤ 105 µA clamp current at ±12 V (MCP6004 abs max from memory).
- EU-P2-13 PASS — gate in = Fig 2.4 (threshold ≈ 0.75 V, README says 0.4 V), CV out = Fig 6.1 (0–5.00 V); 3.3 V-only pins 28/29/30 bounded.
- EU-P2-14 JUDGMENT/PASS — MIDI Type A, IN has D3 (datasheet omits it), OUT/THRU 10R/10R at 3.3 V (8.7 mA); THRU makes the H11L1 sink 21 mA when low (H11L1 limits from memory).

- EU-P2-16 PASS — USB-C data-only with 5.1 k Rd, USBLC6, grounded shell.
- EU-P2-17 JUDGMENT — 5 ERC power_pin_not_driven: no PWR_FLAGs (as Trey's R3-P2-14).
