Sheet notes on the power sheet: "Seed VIN RLC Filter" and, beside FB1, **"idk if we need this extra FB pi filter with everything else going on.."** — Electrosmith's own doubt about the fifth bead, left in the shipped files.

Answers to the scoping questions:
- **Power entry.** 9 V centre-negative barrel; **reverse polarity = one series Schottky (D5)**; no bridge, no shunt diode, no fuse, no NTC. (The Rev4 CDN BOM lists `D5 D6` NSR1020 ×2 — Rev4 adds a second diode in a topology these Rev3 files do not show.)
- **The bead chain, exactly:** FB10 → FB4 → FB5 (three in series at the jack) → D5 → FB9 (fourth, after the diode) → split node. Part: Chilisin **PBY160808T-601Y-N, 600 Ω @ 100 MHz, 1 A, 200 mΩ, 0603**. Bulk: C21 100 µF + C22 100 nF at the diode anode (after the three beads), C13 100 µF at the split node, then 100 µF after each 3R3 (C41, C36 on the Seed branch; C14, C23, C24 on the analog branch), plus C27 100 µF + C26 10 µF at the LDO input and C43 100 µF + C18 10 µF + C15 10 µF at its output. All 100 µF are 35 V SMD electrolytics (ROQANG RVT1V101M0607 per the Rev4 BOM; `CP_Elec_6.3x7.7`).
- **What makes +5 V:** U2 LP2985-5.0 (150 mA LDO) from +9V_A through R57, for the expression buffer and the expression pedal only. **Nothing makes 3.3 V** — +3V3 / +3V3_A are the Seed3's.
- **What feeds Seed VIN:** +9V through R30 / C41 / R6 / C36 — the datasheet Fig 1.1 DSY_VIN branch verbatim.
- **± rails:** none; the audio op-amps run single-supply on +9V_A, biased at ≈ +9V_A / 2 by the MCP6002 buffer U1A (`AREF_AUDIO_IN_BIAS`, sheet note "~4.5V ref for audio bias"; a second note says "This bias would ideally be 9VA / 2, but the 5VA signal will be much more noise resiliant" — the netlist implements the 9VA/2 version).
- **Codec rails:** on the Seed3 module (TAC5242 at 3.3 V). The audio sheet still annotates the Seed's converter as "AK4558 In Range 0.8 * AVDD Vpp = 2.64Vpp" / "Out Range 0.76 * AVDD = 2.508Vpp" — the Seed 1 codec, a stale note; the Seed3 datasheet gives ±1.8 V / 3.6 Vpp in and 0 dBFS = 1 Vrms out.

**This is Trey's rev-3 input chain, element for element** (rev-3 PASS2 §1): kit FB10/FB4/FB5 ≡ Trey FB3/FB4/FB5; kit C21/C22 ≡ C15/C16; kit D5 ≡ **D6**; kit FB9 ≡ FB6; kit C13 ≡ C19 (split node); kit R30/C41/R6/C36 ≡ R29/C17/R30/C18 → DSY_VIN; kit R48/C14/R55/C23+C24 ≡ R31/C20/R32/C21+C22 → `/+9V_FLAG`. Trey's additions: the D2–D5 bridge in front of the first bead, FB7 + the TPS62172 buck and the LEDs on the second branch instead of three op-amps and an LDO. See §11.

- **Inrush.** 9 V side ≈ 830 µF total: **200 µF (C20, C21, C22, C13) behind only the four beads (0.8 Ω DCR nominal, saturating) and D5**; C41/C14 behind 3.3 Ω (2.6 A single pulse into a 0.75 W 1206), C36/C23/C24 behind 6.6 Ω, C27/C26 behind 9.9 Ω. Stored energy 34 mJ. Peak from a stiff 9 V adapter ≈ (9 − 0.5) / (0.6 Ω + ESR + Zout) ≈ 7–12 A into C21, ≈ 9 A into C13 through D5 — versus D5's **5 A 8.3 ms surge / 2 A IFRM**. A wall adapter's current limit and the 100 µF electrolytic ESR (a few hundred mΩ) are the only limiters. **JUDGMENT (PD-P2-03)** — rev-3 R3-P2-05 is **inherited from this reference**: same 200 µF unlimited, same diode; Trey's 50 mΩ beads limit 4× less than the kit's 200 mΩ ones.
- Series-element placement (`§E`): J8 (166.5, 69.1); FB10 / FB4 / FB5 stacked at x = 177.9, y = 61.0 / 62.7 / 64.4 (1.7 mm links); D5 (180.7, 70.8); FB9 (180.7, 74.1); C21 (175.6, 70.2); C13 (180.0, 84.5); analog π R48 → C14 → R55 → C23/C24 along y ≈ 80–85, x 155–176; LDO block R57 → C27 → U2 → C15 → C43 → C18 → FB1 → C19 at x 190–213. **The Seed branch (R30, C41, R6, C36) is 110 mm away at the far left (x 55–63, y 95–103), next to the Seed at (52.8, 125.3)**: +9V runs 144 mm (119.8 mm @ 0.6096 on B.Cu) across the board to it. ## 6. Buttons, toggles, footswitches, pots, expression

**Switches** (`§D`): TAC_SW4 (SW4 pads 1+2 → A1.1 = D0, 98.6–105.1 mm, 1 via), TAC_SW5 (SW5 → A1.35 = D28, 110–117 mm), TOG_SW1_A/B (SW1 ON-OFF-ON → A1.9 = D8 / A1.8 = D7, 24.8 / 33.4 mm), TOG_SW2 (SW2 → A1.10 = D9, 37.9 mm, 0.2 mm), TOG_SW3 (SW3 → **A1.29 = D22, 3.3 V-only pin** — a switch to GND with the internal pull-up, so bounded), GPIO_FSW_1 (FSW_1 → A1.11 = D10, 116.5 mm), GPIO_FSW_2 (FSW_2 → **A1.30 = D23, 3.3 V-only** — likewise a contact to GND, 147.0 mm). JUDGMENT (PD-P2-13). **Pots.** VR1–VR6 Alpha RV09 10 k B: pin 3 → +3V3_A, pin 1 → GND, wiper **directly** to A1.22–A1.27 (ADC0–ADC5 = D15–D20), runs 78.6 / 100.0 / 121.6 / 97.8 / 118.1 / 138.2 mm, 1 via each. PASS (PD-P2-12) — but see PD-P2-06: the rail these six pots hang on has no capacitor.

**Expression input** (datasheet Fig 4.5 verbatim): J6 ring ← R46 100 Ω ← +5V_A (pot supply); J6 tip → `EXPRESSION_IN_EXT` (R18 1 M → GND) → U1.5; U1B unity buffer (U1.7 = U1.6 = `ADC_EXPRESSION_PRE`) → R17 10 k → `ADC_EXPRESSION` [R60 20 k → GND, C46 10 nF → GND] → **A1.28 (ADC6 = D21, 3.3 V-only pin 28)**; J6 tip-normal → R68 10 k → GND (unplugged = 0 V). Divider 20/30 = 0.667, pole 2.4 kHz, runs 145 mm (jack → U1) and 10–13 mm (divider → Seed).

- Full-scale: 5.00 V × 0.667 = **3.333 V**; with the LP2985's +1.5 % tolerance band and a rail-to-rail VOH, **3.38 V** at the ADC pin. Datasheet Table 3 gives pin 28's expected operating range as 0 V … +3V3 (l.280) and Table 1 excepts pin 28 from the 5 V tolerance (l.130–136); the STM32's own abs max for a non-tolerant pin is VDDA + 0.3 ≈ 3.6 V (model memory). So: 1–2.5 % over the datasheet's operating ceiling, 0.2 V under the silicon abs max, and the datasheet's own text says operating-range excursions "will not necessarily damage" the Seed (l.269–270). **ZERO-MARGIN (PD-P2-11)** — and it is Electrosmith's own Fig 4.5 circuit (the v2.1 changelog "fixed op-amp power in expression input circuit" moved the buffer onto +5V_A; the ratio stayed 2/3). A 15 k / 20 k or 10 k / 18 k divider would land at 2.86 / 3.21 V.
- MCP6002 on +5V_A: tip voltage ≤ 5 V (the pot's own supply) → inside VDD + 1 V (model memory). The bias buffer U1A outputs ≈ 4.2 V on a 5 V rail — inside VOH.

**Explicit statement for the Oct-7 question.** Trey's four-bead chain (FB3/FB4/FB5 → D6 → FB6) + the two 3.3 Ω / 100 µF / 3.3 Ω / 100 µF π-filters **is the Pedal kit's FB10/FB4/FB5 → D5 → FB9 + R30/C41/R6/C36 and R48/C14/R55/C23/C24 chain, which is in turn the Seed3 datasheet's Fig 1.1, copied component for component** — including the 100 nF + 100 µF at the diode anode and the 100 µF at the split node. The kit's bead is the Chilisin PBY160808T-601Y-N, **rated 1 A** (600 Ω @ 100 MHz, 200 mΩ), as the Oct-7 review said; the Rev3 files themselves do not name it (value `FERRITE_BEAD_0603`), the Rev4 CDN BOM and the Eurorack Rev3 BOM do. What Trey changed: (1) a Schottky bridge ahead of the chain while keeping the series diode (the kit has only the series diode); (2) the bead part (2 A / 120 Ω / 50 mΩ instead of 1 A / 600 Ω / 200 mΩ); (3) 2512 1 W instead of 1206 0.75 W for the 3.3 Ω; (4) the second branch feeds a 0.22–0.30 A buck + LEDs instead of 17 mA of op-amps — the load, not the circuit, is why D6 runs at its rating and `/+9V_FLAG` sags on his board and not on the kit.

## Summary (one line per finding)

- PD-P2-01 PASS — power entry is datasheet Fig 1.1 verbatim: FB10/FB4/FB5 → NSR1020 D5 (sole polarity protection) → FB9 → 100 µF split → two 3R3/100 µF/3R3/100 µF π-filters; beads are Chilisin PBY160808T-601Y-N 600 Ω / **1 A** / 200 mΩ; = Trey's chain minus his bridge.
- PD-P2-02 PASS — DSY_VIN 6.4–7.9 V, +9V_A 8.2–8.5 V, LP2985 at 1 % load with datasheet caps; beads/D5 ≤ 32 % of 1 A; 3R3 ≤ 40 % of 0.75 W; D5 ≤ 65 % of PF (Seed current assumed 0.1–0.3 A).
- PD-P2-03 JUDGMENT — 200 µF behind only beads + D5, ≈ 830 µF total, 34 mJ, 7–12 A peak vs a 5 A surge rating: R3-P2-05 is inherited from this reference.
- PD-P2-04 JUDGMENT — the kit protects with one series diode; Trey's bridge is the addition and D6 the copy, run at 1.5–4× the kit's current (≈ 100 % of PF).
- PD-P2-05 JUDGMENT — one 100 nF on +3V3 at the opto, 209 mm routed from the Seed; SD socket 239 mm from any cap (31 mm from the Seed).
- PD-P2-06 JUDGMENT — +3V3_A has no capacitor at all (six pots on the Seed's bare output).
- PD-P2-07 PASS — OPA1652 / LP2985 / MCP6002 / bias-node decoupling all per datasheet.
- PD-P2-08 PASS — 16/24 mil power copper, ≤ 32 % IPC utilisation; Seed-VIN π-section placed at the Seed, 110 mm from the entry.
- PD-P2-09 JUDGMENT — GPIO-sourced 5 mm LEDs through 1 k / 2 k: 0.17–1.4 mA.
- PD-P2-10 ZERO-MARGIN — green/blue Vf ≈ 3.3 V against a 3.3 V GPIO through 2 k (Kingbright sheet unreachable).
- PD-P2-11 ZERO-MARGIN — expression input (datasheet Fig 4.5) puts 3.33–3.38 V on 3.3 V-only pin 28 (operating ceiling 3.3 V; abs max ≈ 3.6 V from memory).
- PD-P2-12 PASS — six pots direct to ADC0–5, pins 24/25 bounded by +3V3_A.
- PD-P2-13 JUDGMENT — nine unfiltered switch inputs on internal pull-ups, footswitch runs 116–148 mm; README refdes differ from the files.
- PD-P2-14 PASS — audio chain as datasheet Fig 3.1–3.3 with ≈ 4.2 V DC bias on the Seed's AC-coupled inputs; stale AK4558 / "G = 1.6" sheet notes.
- PD-P2-15 JUDGMENT — MIDI IN has no anti-parallel diode (the Euro kit, Pod and Trey all fit one).
- PD-P2-16 JUDGMENT — MIDI THRU makes the H11L1 sink 21 mA when low (limits from memory).
- PD-P2-17 PASS — USB-C data-only with 5.1 k Rd, USBLC6, grounded shell; ERC has PWR_FLAGs.
- PD-P2-18 JUDGMENT — Rev1-named sub-sheets under a Rev3 root, Rev3 BOM without MPNs, bead value without a part: the files do not tell a copier what bead rating they are copying.
