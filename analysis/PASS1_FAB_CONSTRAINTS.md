## Notes
- **Row 1** is the board's defining fab risk: 3.85 meters of trace at exactly the minimum width, with JLCPCB's stated ±20% width tolerance. Not a spec violation — but zero margin across the majority of the routing, including the +3V3_D power rail. Recommendation (JUDGMENT): widen to 0.2–0.25mm where congestion allows; 2oz copper would raise the minimum to 0.16mm and make current widths illegal.
- **Rows 4, 7, 9** are technically below spec but the boards fabbed successfully twice — JLCPCB's tolerance absorbed them. They are latent risks (a different fab lot, or any fab, may reject or produce weak barrels/exposed edge copper), not observed failures.
- Unused drill-tool definitions (0.4, 0.6, 0.8, 1.5mm at 0 hits) are harmless plot artifacts.
- Gerber has **93** vias; the `.kicad_pcb` has **94** — one-via delta consistent with the F-015 rerouting between versions (F-017).
- Min copper spacing (row 2) is measured from the `.kicad_pcb`; the Gerber pour's clearance to tracks was generated by a zone definition not present in our file — it passed JLCPCB DFM twice, taken as fab-verified.
